# Reviewer response: measurement timing

> Synthetic editing example. The reviewer question and manuscript changes below are invented for a language-editing demonstration. They do not refer to a real submission.

## Reviewer question

Were the sensor reading and the task score collected at the same time? Please clarify the timing and its implications for interpretation.

## Response

We sincerely thank the reviewer for this insightful and constructive comment, which has provided an invaluable opportunity to enhance the clarity and rigor of our manuscript. As described in the Introduction, classroom measurements can be considered alongside task performance, and the study examined this relationship in 72 classrooms. We fully agree that the timing of measurement is an important issue that warrants careful and comprehensive consideration. In response to this comment, we have now specified in Methods, paragraph 2, that the sensor reading and task score were collected at the end of the same teaching session. We have also added to Discussion, paragraph 3, that these simultaneous measurements cannot determine whether a change in the reading preceded a change in task performance. These revisions not only clarify the measurement procedure but also strengthen the transparency and interpretability of the study. We hope that the revised text satisfactorily addresses the reviewer's concern and contributes to a clearer understanding of our findings.
