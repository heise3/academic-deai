# Reviewer response: measurement timing

> Synthetic editing example. The reviewer question and manuscript changes below are invented for a language-editing demonstration. They do not refer to a real submission.

## Reviewer question

Were the sensor reading and the task score collected at the same time? Please clarify the timing and its implications for interpretation.

## Response

Yes. In the 72 classrooms, the sensor reading and task score were collected simultaneously at the end of the same teaching session. We have specified this timing in Methods, paragraph 2. We have also added to Discussion, paragraph 3, that these simultaneous measurements cannot determine whether a change in the reading preceded a change in task performance.
