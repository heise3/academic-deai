## Humanizer

The contextual editing guidance in `SKILL.md`, `references/source-patterns.md`, and `references/voice-and-context.md` draws on and adapts ideas from **Humanizer 3.1.0** by **Siqi Chen (blader)**.

- Repository: https://github.com/blader/humanizer
- Reviewed commit: `225a6f39ac85f76ee48dbad772ea4abe4ed6c9d8`
- Reviewed source: https://github.com/blader/humanizer/blob/225a6f39ac85f76ee48dbad772ea4abe4ed6c9d8/SKILL.md
- Upstream license: MIT; the original notice is preserved in [licenses/humanizer-MIT.txt](licenses/humanizer-MIT.txt).
- Review date: 2026-10-03.

Adaptations prioritize structural redundancy, contextual weak signals, author samples, and genuine contrasts. They retain the academic editor's evidence protections. They do not import upstream's default draft/final output pair, permission to invent reactions, or blanket punctuation deletion.

The upstream prompt and its full example library are not bundled. DownAI's new academic examples are invented teaching examples, not research data.

## Project and local baseline

Academic DeAI 3.0.0 is the successor of the locally maintained DownAI 2.2 editing package. Its text tools and academic references are retained, with updated contextual editing guidance and publishing resources.

The maintainer authorized selecting the release license; this project is distributed under [MIT](LICENSE). The local baseline had no standalone license file. No private source corpus is bundled, and no external package code is vendored. Humanizer's upstream notice is retained separately rather than replacing its copyright attribution.
